KEPT-PL-016 — MVP behavioural reward formula and settlement

Date: 08-Sep-2026
Title: Behavioural reward calculation and payout destination
Status: ACCEPTED
Decision: For qualified MVP commitments, RewardController calculates rewards from capped time-weighted eligible balance × epoch-specific reward rate × verification confidence × reward weight, then applies per-qualification, per-user-per-epoch and total-epoch caps. Qualification is binary for MVP; no partial completion scalar is introduced. Deployment-wide immutable hard ceilings bound all configurable epoch values. Rewards are claimable as native Monad USDC to the user's pre-bound Privy Monad wallet and are not automatically converted into vault shares.
Reason: This preserves the Product Outline's time-weighted/confidence-based economics while keeping the backend from specifying arbitrary payouts, bounding oracle compromise and maintaining strict financial separation between saver principal and behavioural treasury.
Alternatives: Backend-specified payout; continuous partial-completion rewards; automatic vault-share rewards; fixed flat reward.
Affected teams: Contracts, Backend, Economics, Privacy/Security, Product/UX, QA.
Implementation consequence: RewardController performs bounded fixed-point calculation; Backend submits qualification inputs rather than payout amounts; UX treats earned reward as separate USDC reward; Economics must supply concrete hard ceilings and epoch values.
Context file to update: Kept-MVP-Technical-Architecture-07-Sep-2026.txt, Kept-Behavioural-Reward-Economics-Framework-07-Sep-2026.txt, backend contract-facing spec, smart-contract acceptance criteria.

DECISION ID: KEPT-PL-017

Date: 08-Sep-2026
Title: Vault, strategy binding and emergency pause semantics
Status: ACCEPTED
Decision: KeptSavingsVault retains the standard ERC-4626 deposit, mint, withdraw and redeem paths. The vault is deployed before the strategy, the AaveUSDCStrategy is deployed with the vault as its immutable sole asset-moving caller, and the vault then binds that strategy exactly once before accepting deposits. The strategy address cannot subsequently be changed. Kept-level pausing blocks new deposits, mints and automatic deposits but never blocks withdraw or redeem.
Reason: This avoids CREATE2/predicted-address deployment complexity while still giving the final deployment a permanently bound strategy. Preserving withdrawal during Kept pause enforces the requirement that saver principal not be trapped by an operational control.
Alternatives: Constructor-time cyclic deployment; runtime-switchable strategy; globally paused ERC-4626; disabled mint/redeem functions.
Affected teams: Contracts, Security, Wallet/Intents, QA.
Implementation consequence: Vault has one write-once strategy binding operation; strategy has immutable vault authority; deposit paths require the strategy to be bound; withdrawal remains available during Kept pause.

DECISION ID: KEPT-PL-018

Date: 08-Sep-2026
Title: RewardController roles and weekly epoch model
Status: ACCEPTED
Decision: RewardController uses OpenZeppelin AccessControl with separate qualification, epoch-configuration and pause roles. Reward epochs are sequential, non-overlapping seven-day periods opened by the epoch manager. Epoch configuration is immutable once opened. Reward USDC must already be available before an epoch opens. Anyone may add reward USDC; no role can withdraw reward USDC or rescue arbitrary tokens in the MVP.
Reason: Separate roles preserve bounded authority while seven-day epochs align directly with the three weekly MVP commitment paths and eliminate unnecessary annualized/epoch-duration arithmetic. A no-withdraw reward treasury provides the simplest solvency guarantee for the small MVP budget.
Alternatives: Ownable-only RewardController; 28-day reward epochs; mutable active epochs; treasury withdrawal/recovery authority; role-gated funding.
Affected teams: Contracts, Backend, Security, Economics, QA.
Implementation consequence: One current reward epoch at a time; fixed seven-day duration; pre-funding check at epoch creation; no treasury recovery function.
Context file to update: docs/specs/MVP Contract & Reward Specification.md; Product Lead Decision Log; reward economics spec.

DECISION ID: KEPT-PL-019

Title: Qualification registration and reward-claim semantics
Status: ACCEPTED
Decision: The onchain replay key is a non-semantic bytes32 qualificationId. Only qualified outcomes are submitted onchain; absence of a qualification record represents no onchain reward qualification. DECISION ID: KEPT-PL-022

Date: 14-Sep-2026
Title: Bounded platform yield fees
Status: ACCEPTED
Decision: Each Kept savings vault may retain a transparent fee only from positive strategy yield by minting vault shares to an immutable Kept fee recipient. The standard Aave vault fee is the lesser of a 100-basis-point annualized charge on its checkpointed, flow-adjusted fee basis or 2,500 basis points of eligible positive yield. The enhanced stablecoin-LP vault fee is the lesser of a 200-basis-point annualized charge on its checkpointed, flow-adjusted fee basis or 2,000 basis points of eligible positive yield. For each elapsed interval, the annualized limit uses the lower of the prior checkpoint basis and the currently observed asset balance rather than claiming to reconstruct an unobservable continuous balance path. High-water-mark accounting prevents fees during losses or recovery. Deposits, employer or sponsor contributions, withdrawals and transfers are not yield. Direct unsolicited asset donations are eligible positive yield because the current strategy boundary cannot distinguish them safely. No fee may be taken from principal, no administrator may mint an arbitrary fee or sweep saver assets, and behavioural rewards remain separately pre-funded through RewardController.
Reason: Kept needs recurring vault revenue while preserving user principal, aligning platform revenue with realized performance and presenting users with a simple net variable rate.
Alternatives: No vault revenue; an uncapped AUM management fee; a pure percentage-of-performance fee; Kept administrator withdrawal authority; using saver yield directly for behavioural rewards.
Affected teams: Contracts, Product/UX, Economics, Security, Backend/Indexing, Wallet/Intents, QA.
Implementation consequence: Fee recipient and fee ceilings are immutable per deployment. The current Aave-backed KeptSavingsVault is the standard profile and must hard-cap configuration at 100 annual basis points and 2,500 positive-yield basis points. Fee shares are created only by deterministic vault accounting, explicit fee crystallization is permissionless, and every deposit, mint, withdrawal and redemption crystallizes any eligible fee before its ERC-4626 share conversion. Fee accounting must be neutral to capital flows, round in savers' favour, reset safely when the vault becomes empty, and receive unit, fuzz, invariant and Monad-fork coverage. The selected enhanced strategy, its 200/2,000 fee profile and its final topology remain blocked on the additional specification and review required by KEPT-PL-021.
Context file to update: docs/specs/MVP Contract & Reward Specification.md; product scope and UX specifications; deployment configuration and consumer fee disclosures.
