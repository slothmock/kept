KEPT HANDOFF #1
From: QA / Hackathon / Release Lead
To: Product Lead
Type: RISK
Priority: HIGH

Summary:
The supplied Social, Attention & Culture track description says projects belong when the core user value is social connection, cultural participation, or community. Kept's current source-of-truth thesis makes the core value behavioural savings, with social verification only one of three verification paths. That creates a genuine track-fit risk.

Recommendation:
Do not distort Kept into a social-first product merely to fit the track. Product Lead should verify whether another primary hackathon track is available/better aligned while retaining the Aurora and Privy bounties. If Social, Attention & Culture remains the chosen primary track, submission copy must make an evidence-based case for accountability/community as core user value without falsely rewriting Kept's thesis.

Why:
The hackathon track's own stated inclusion test and Kept's product thesis do not cleanly match. Quietly reframing Kept would violate project change control and weaken the product.

Alternatives considered:

Reposition Kept as social-first — rejected as inconsistent with current product thesis.
Ignore the track language and submit unchanged — unnecessarily exposes the project to eligibility/judging risk.
Select a better-fitting primary track while retaining sponsor bounties — preferred if hackathon rules permit.

Cross-team impact:

Product/UX: Do not artificially increase public/social functionality.
Contracts: None.
Wallet/Intents: None.
Backend: None.
Privacy/Security: Avoid weakening privacy to make social features more visible.
Economics/Regulatory: None immediate.
QA/Release: Submission narrative and judging strategy may change.

Decision required from Product Lead:
Confirm the intended primary hackathon track and whether Social, Attention & Culture remains the submission target.

Project-context update required:
Hackathon/submission plan and, if necessary, Product Outline hackathon-context section.
