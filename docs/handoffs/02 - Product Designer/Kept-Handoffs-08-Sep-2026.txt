KEPT HANDOFF #1

From: Product Designer / UX Lead
To: Product Lead
Type: DECISION
Priority: HIGH

Summary:
The MVP contract surface can be reduced to KeptSavingsVault, AaveUSDCStrategy behind IYieldStrategy, and RewardController. CommitmentRegistry is redundant because commitment definitions, lifecycle, proof and trust state are already private/offchain, while RewardController can hold the minimal qualification information required for real onchain reward settlement.

Recommendation:
Delete CommitmentRegistry from the MVP. Keep the vault and behavioural reward systems completely independent. Make the Aave strategy immutable for the initial vault deployment, use ERC-4626 for savings accounting, and fold the prefunded USDC reward treasury into RewardController.

Why:
This removes duplicated commitment state, reduces privacy leakage, avoids backend/onchain lifecycle synchronization, reduces contract/security surface and still satisfies all three required verification paths and real behavioural reward settlement.

Alternatives considered:

Keep a minimal CommitmentRegistry containing commitment IDs and results.
Put commitment catalogue/type/version data onchain.
Merge behavioural rewards into KeptSavingsVault.
Put Aave integration directly into KeptSavingsVault.

Cross-team impact:

Product/UX: No loss of functionality; fewer blockchain concepts can leak into UX.
Contracts: Three-component deployed architecture instead of four/five; reduced audit surface.
Wallet/Intents: No material change. Funding still terminates at the user's Monad wallet then the vault.
Backend: Backend remains authoritative for commitment lifecycle and must reliably persist/index the commitment-to-onchain-result mapping.
Privacy/Security: Improved; social graph, commitment type and proof context remain offchain.
Economics/Regulatory: Behavioural treasury remains explicitly separate from base yield; reward amount can remain changeable between programme/epoch designs.
QA/Release: Must test reward budget exhaustion, duplicate qualification, duplicate claims and backend/onchain reconciliation; fewer contract integrations overall.

Decision required from Product Lead:
Approve deletion of CommitmentRegistry from MVP and approve the three-component contract architecture: KeptSavingsVault + AaveUSDCStrategy/IYieldStrategy + RewardController.

Project-context update required:
Kept-MVP-Technical-Architecture-07-Sep-2026.txt should remove CommitmentRegistry from the recommended MVP contract graph and specify that minimal commitment qualification state lives directly in RewardController.

OPEN QUESTIONS
Decisions made within role authority: None affecting contract architecture; this is a proposed simplification for Product Lead approval.
Assumptions: ERC-4626 remains the preferred vault standard; MVP reward is paid directly in USDC; backend qualification authority is acceptable for the hackathon; strategy is immutable for the initial deployment.
Blockers: Product Lead approval is required before deleting CommitmentRegistry. Smart-Contract Engineer must review the exact interface and security assumptions before implementation.
Dependencies: Privacy/Security review of qualification authority; Backend agreement on commitment/result identifiers and idempotency; Economics confirmation of the bounded epoch inputs required by RewardController.
Items requiring Product Lead approval: Delete CommitmentRegistry; no runtime strategy switching; RewardController doubles as reward treasury; backend calculates the MVP reward entitlement before submitting the bounded onchain qualification.
