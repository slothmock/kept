KEPT HANDOFF # 1
From: Monad / Smart-Contract Engineer
To: Product Lead
Type: DECISION
Priority: HIGH

Summary:
The MVP smart-contract graph can be frozen at three deployed contracts: ERC-4626 KeptSavingsVault, single immutable/bound AaveUSDCStrategy, and financially isolated RewardController. CommitmentRegistry, strategy management, separate treasury, verifier/social contracts and a separate automation contract should not be deployed. The design implements accepted decisions KEPT-PL-004 through PL-010 and PL-012.

Recommendation:
Approve the contract boundary above. Use OpenZeppelin ERC-4626 for saver accounting; bind the vault permanently to one Aave strategy for this deployment; keep rewards completely financially isolated; and use an opaque commitmentId plus bounded qualification inputs in RewardController.

For the MVP reward calculation, approve a period-rate formula based on time-weighted eligible balance × completion × confidence × reward weight, with immutable deployment-wide hard ceilings and epoch-specific lower caps.

Why:
It provides standard saver accounting, keeps Aave replaceable at the architectural boundary without runtime migration complexity, prevents reward/oracle compromise from touching saver funds, enforces the accepted seven-day automatic-saving cadence onchain, minimizes behavioural metadata, and removes unnecessary synchronization/admin surfaces.

Alternatives considered:

Custom non-ERC-4626 vault.
Aave integrated directly into the vault.
Runtime-switchable strategy.
Standalone CommitmentRegistry.
Standalone reward treasury.
Backend-submitted arbitrary USDC reward amount.
Fully onchain commitment verification.
Separate automatic-savings contract.

Cross-team impact:

Product/UX: Standard deposit/withdraw behaviour; automatic saving has one dedicated action; reward claim is separate from saver principal.
Contracts: Three deployed contracts; no proxy; no strategy switching.
Wallet/Intents: Privy policy should whitelist depositAutomatically(uint256) specifically; Aurora remains two-stage into user Monad USDC first.
Backend: Must produce opaque commitmentId, epochId, time-weighted eligible balance, completion, confidence and reward weight.
Privacy/Security: No verifier identity or semantic commitment type enters contract state; review role assignment and immutable limits.
Economics/Regulatory: Must provide deployment values for epoch/reward hard ceilings and confirm period-rate economics.
QA/Release: Fork tests can target one fixed Aave strategy and adversarially test oracle/automatic-saving bounds.

Decision required from Product Lead:
Approve or reject the proposed MVP RewardController formula: time-weighted eligible balance × epoch reward rate × completion × confidence × reward weight, subject to per-user, per-qualification and epoch-budget caps. Also confirm that MVP behavioural rewards are claimable as native USDC to the user's wallet rather than automatically minted into vault shares.

Project-context update required:
Kept-MVP-Technical-Architecture-07-Sep-2026.txt should replace the obsolete four-module contract graph with this three-contract graph and exact authority boundary. Smart-contract acceptance criteria should incorporate these interfaces.
